Numbers don't lie, but signals do their best to. I am a PhD candidate at the University of L'Aquila, and my research is focused on analysing numerical methods for signal processing. In particular, I develop and test algorithms for decomposing nonstationary signals to understand the information contained in them. From there, I apply the same thinking to acoustic beamforming for tracking noise back to its source, mainly in wind turbines and automotive systems. In simpler terms, I analyse data 😁.
